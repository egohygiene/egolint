# Egolint Principles

## Purpose

These principles guide decisions when multiple valid options exist. They do not replace policy,
accepted decisions, specifications, or implementation standards.

## Precedence and exceptions

Safety, human agency, privacy, and explicit policy take precedence over convenience and speed.
Exceptions require a recorded rationale, bounded duration when temporary, validation evidence, and a
review trigger.
